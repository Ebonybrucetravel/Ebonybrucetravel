## 3. Services

### 3.1 Tours and non-flight ticket operations
The website promotes tour bookings and non-flight ticket operations.

**Published description:** Customers can discover tours and arrange ticket operations with support intended to make travel planning easier.

**AI/customer-support handling:**
- Ask what destination or experience the customer has in mind.
- Ask for intended travel dates, number of travellers, and any relevant preferences.
- Collect the customer's contact details for a quotation or follow-up.
- Do not invent tour packages, prices, inclusions, availability, cancellation terms, or destination coverage.
- Refer requests requiring confirmation to the travel operations team.

**Details requiring confirmation:** Destinations served, available tour products, suppliers, inclusions, exclusions, booking lead times, payment methods, refund rules, and whether the company offers guided or customised tours.

### 3.2 Hotel reservations
The website advertises hotel bookings and says it aims to provide competitive rates and comfortable stays worldwide.

**AI/customer-support handling:**
Collect:
- Destination/city
- Check-in and check-out dates
- Number of adults and children
- Number of rooms
- Preferred hotel category or budget, if supplied
- Any accessibility or other important requirements

Explain that a team member must confirm live availability, final rates, taxes, payment terms, and booking conditions before a reservation is treated as confirmed.

**Do not:** Promise a room, quote an unverified price, or state that a booking is confirmed without confirmation from the booking system or operations team.

### 3.3 DHL-franchised logistics services
The website advertises DHL-franchised logistics services. Its FAQ describes services including:
- Courier services
- International parcel shipping
- Document handling
- Package tracking

**AI/customer-support handling:**
Ask whether the enquiry concerns sending a parcel, sending documents, or tracking an existing shipment.

For a new shipment, collect only the information needed for a staff member to assist, such as origin, destination, parcel/document type, approximate weight and dimensions, and preferred timing. Do not estimate a final shipping price or delivery date unless an authorised live DHL rate/quote source provides it.

For tracking, request the shipment's tracking/reference number through an appropriate private channel. Do not publish customer shipment details in public replies.

**Details requiring confirmation:** Accepted and restricted shipment categories, packaging requirements, service areas, collection/drop-off options, rates, insurance, delivery estimates, claims process, and official tracking workflow.

### 3.4 Educational consulting and admission assistance
The website promotes educational consulting and “Speedy Admission Processing.” It describes support with student admission and academic guidance.

The Contact page explicitly states that the company **does not provide immigration or visa representation services**. It says educational consulting focuses on academic counselling and course matching only.

**AI/customer-support handling:**
- Ask which country, institution, course, and intended intake the student is considering.
- Ask about the student's current education level and relevant academic interests.
- Explain that the service is educational guidance/admission support, not immigration or visa representation.
- Do not guarantee admission, scholarships, visas, employment, or a particular outcome.
- Refer eligibility, application requirements, fees, deadlines, and document checks to the education-consulting team.

**Details requiring confirmation:** Countries and institutions supported, course categories, application fees, consulting fees, document requirements, timelines, partner institutions, and the exact boundary between admission support and other services.

### 3.5 Flight booking — scope needs confirmation
The About page refers to flight-booking services, and the Contact-page FAQ answers “Do you book flights?” with “Yes.” However, the Services page labels the tour/ticket offering as non-flight operations.

Until the company confirms the current operating model, an AI assistant should acknowledge the enquiry and route it to staff rather than promise that a flight can be booked.

**Details requiring confirmation:** Airline inventory source, booking channels, supported routes, ticket issuance process, service fees, payment methods, change/refund handling, baggage support, and after-sales responsibilities.

## 6. Customer enquiry and lead-capture workflow

A general first-response workflow:

1. **Welcome the customer.** Ask how the team can help.
2. **Identify the service.** Categorise the enquiry as tours/non-flight tickets, hotel reservations, DHL logistics, educational consulting/admission, flight booking, or other.
3. **Collect relevant details.** Ask only for information needed for that service.
4. **Capture contact details.** Obtain the customer's name and preferred contact method. Collect sensitive documents only through an approved, secure company process.
5. **Set expectations.** Explain that availability, pricing, eligibility, and final terms may need confirmation by the relevant team.
6. **Route the enquiry.** Send it to the appropriate operations or customer-service queue.
7. **Record the outcome.** Track new enquiry, awaiting information, assigned, quotation sent, awaiting payment, confirmed, completed, or closed.
8. **Follow up appropriately.** Follow the company's approved response-time and consent rules.

### Suggested enquiry fields
- Customer name
- Email address
- Phone/WhatsApp number
- Country of residence (if relevant)
- Service requested
- Destination or institution (if relevant)
- Travel dates or intake (if relevant)
- Number of travellers/rooms (if relevant)
- Enquiry details
- Preferred contact channel
- Consent to receive follow-up messages
- Assigned team/owner
- Enquiry status
- Date received and last follow-up date

Do not request passport numbers, full payment-card details, passwords, or other highly sensitive information in an ordinary website chat or public social-media message.

## 10. Potential AI and business automation opportunities

These are recommendations, not confirmed existing company capabilities.

### Website AI assistant
- Answer basic questions using this knowledge base.
- Identify which service a visitor needs.
- Collect structured enquiry details.
- Route enquiries to the appropriate team.
- Escalate complex, urgent, or sensitive requests to a human.

### Lead management
- Send website enquiries to a central CRM or lead sheet.
- Assign each enquiry to a service category and staff owner.
- Notify the responsible team when a new enquiry arrives.
- Track response times, follow-ups, quotations, and conversions.

### Email automation
- Send an acknowledgement after a form submission.
- Notify the relevant service team.
- Send a follow-up when an enquiry has not received a response, subject to company policy.
- Send booking or admission-process updates only when supported by verified operational data.

### Hotel and travel operations
- Use structured forms to collect dates, destinations, room requirements, and traveller counts.
- Connect to authorised inventory and booking systems before displaying live prices or availability.
- Send booking details and reminders only after a booking is confirmed.

### DHL enquiry handling
- Categorise shipment and tracking enquiries.
- Route tracking requests to the approved tracking workflow.
- Avoid exposing shipment details to unauthorised people.

### Marketing and email campaigns
- Segment contacts by service interest and consent.
- Share relevant travel, hotel, logistics, and education updates.
- Use clear unsubscribe options and comply with applicable privacy and electronic-marketing requirements.
- Measure enquiries, qualified leads, bookings, and campaign conversions.

- Treat this file as a starting point, not as authority to make commercial commitments.
